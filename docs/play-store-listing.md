## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Hush Tinnitus is a calm, evidence-informed companion for tinnitus management —
support for people living with the perception of ringing, buzzing, or hissing
in the ears. It brings together sound therapy, guided breathing, and
CBT-informed education in one free app, with no account required to get
started.

SOUND THERAPY & MASKING
Choose from synthesised white, pink, and brown noise, or nature soundscapes
like rain, ocean waves, and a crackling fire — all useful for tinnitus masking
and everyday sound enrichment. Pitch-matching helps you find the frequency
closest to your own tinnitus, and notched therapy applies a targeted filter
around that pitch for a more tailored listening experience. Set a session
timer with a gentle fade-out, and let audio keep playing in the background —
through calls, screen lock, and other apps.

BREATHING & RELAXATION
Slow your body down with 4-7-8 breathing and box breathing, both fully free,
with an animated visual guide to help you pace each breath.

LEARN
Understand the neuroscience behind tinnitus in plain language, including the
habituation model — how attention and stress can amplify what you perceive,
and how sound enrichment and consistent daily habits can help your brain learn
to filter it into the background over time. Includes a sleep hygiene guide,
a noise exposure reference chart, and a clear red flag guide covering symptoms
that warrant a prompt conversation with your GP or audiologist.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Log your daily loudness and distress levels, and track sessions over time.
The CREST assessment — a compact 12-question in-app symptom tracker developed
by an audiologist — gives you a simple 0–100 score across six areas of daily
life, so you can see how things are trending and share a one-tap PDF summary
with your audiologist at your next appointment.

WHY HUSH TINNITUS
- Full core features free, no account needed
- Persistent background audio — keeps playing during calls and screen lock
- No aggressive paywalls or hidden costs
- Built with an audiologist, grounded in tinnitus management research
- Works fully offline — all core sounds available without internet
- On-device data storage — your logs and scores stay on your device

Hush Tinnitus is a self-management tool and is not a medical device. It does
not diagnose, treat, or cure tinnitus or any medical condition. Always consult
a qualified healthcare professional — a GP, audiologist, or ENT specialist —
before making changes to how you manage your tinnitus.
```

## Setup recommendations for users

For best notification reliability on Android, we recommend setting Hush
Tinnitus to unrestricted battery usage: Settings → Apps → Hush Tinnitus →
Battery → select Unrestricted. This prevents Android's battery optimisation
from delaying or blocking daily reminder notifications.
