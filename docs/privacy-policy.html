Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Hush Tinnitus is built by RESONEAR to support people managing tinnitus, without asking you to hand over your personal data to do it. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Everything you enter into Hush Tinnitus stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no sign-up. We do not see your data, and we do not sell or share it, because we never receive it in the first place.

What data the app collects

Hush Tinnitus only stores information you choose to enter while using the app's self-management tools:

  • Symptom logs — the loudness, distress, time of day, notes, and any trigger tags you record.
  • CREST assessment scores — your responses and results from the in-app tinnitus impact questionnaire.
  • Session history — a record of the sound therapy, breathing, and relaxation sessions you complete, along with your app preferences and settings.

No account, email address, name, or other personal identifier is ever requested or required to use any feature of the app.

Where your data is stored

All of the data listed above is stored locally on your device, in a private SQLite database created and managed by the app itself. It is never transmitted to RESONEAR, to any cloud service, or to any other server — Hush Tinnitus does not have a backend, and it does not need an internet connection to store or use your data.

Exporting your data

You can generate a report of your own data at any time using the Export my data feature in Settings. This creates a file on your device that you choose whether and how to share — for example, with your GP or audiologist. This export happens entirely on your device; RESONEAR does not receive a copy.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

Hush Tinnitus does not include any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking software of any kind. Your usage of the app is not measured, profiled, or monetised by us or by anyone else.

Your control over your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of its data with it. If you'd like to keep a copy first, use the export feature described above before uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if a new feature changes what data the app stores — we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.
